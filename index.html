Victor Dragonclaw
Mythic-Tier Warrior • House Gallant Lineage

Identity & Origin

Victor's designation marks him as one of the few individuals capable of channeling draconic essence without losing their humanity. He is not a shapeshifter or a traditional mage; his abilities stem from a symbiotic bond with an ancestral draconic force that traces back to the Dawn of Creation.

Core Abilities: Draconic Resonance

When tapping into the latent power of his ancestor, Victor experiences amplified strength, heightened perception, and heat-based aura generation. His signature Dragon Claw Manifestation allows him to generate force-constructs shaped like talons to physically tear through magical barriers and shadow-constructs.

Limitations & Overburn

His power hinges on emotional restraint. Prolonged use causes internal overheating, and emotional triggers like fear or rage can lead to an uncontrolled manifestation, making him vulnerable to chaos-inducing forces.
